🎣 Fishy Friends

Catch fish, earn coins, upgrade your gear. Jump into a public server or play with friends using a room code.

Computer: WASD move, mouse look, hold left click to charge and release to cast, click when the bobber dips, hold to reel (let go when the line is tight!).
VR: left stick move, right stick turn, right trigger does the same as left click. Point at the boards and pull a trigger to shop.
