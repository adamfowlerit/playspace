🎣 Fishy Friends

Catch fish, earn coins, upgrade your gear. Jump into a public server or play with friends using a room code.

Computer: WASD move, mouse look, hold left click to charge and release to cast, click when the bobber dips, hold to reel (let go when the line is tight!). Hold V near a 🤖 bot to talk to it.
VR: put your hands on the ground and push or swing your arms to move (fling to jump), or use the left stick to move, right stick turn, right trigger does the same as left click. Point at the boards and pull a trigger to shop or travel. Hold a grip button near a 🤖 bot to talk to it.
